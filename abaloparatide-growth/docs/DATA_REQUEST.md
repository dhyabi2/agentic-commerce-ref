# DATA REQUEST — what to obtain to close the decisive gaps

## 1. Pediatric PTH(1-34) cohort — serial bone ages & final height vs target (highest human value)
**Holders:** Winer/Cutler group (NIH/NICHD) cohorts underlying PMC6298875, PMID 39883563, 20392870.
**Requested (de-identified, per patient):** age, sex, diagnosis (APS-1 vs CaSR), pubertal stage, PTH
dose & dosing frequency, treatment duration, serial **bone age (BA) with chronological age (CA)** and
method, height/height-SDS trajectory, growth-cessation age, **near-final/final height vs mid-parental
target**, mineral labs. **Analysis enabled:** BA/CA tempo (maturation-advance test) and adult-height-
vs-target — the only available human test of whether PTH1R agonism preserves reserve / alters tempo.
**Why needed:** abstracts/full texts do not tabulate serial BA; without it the human
reserve-preservation signature is untestable.

## 2. FDA / EMA / TGA nonclinical packages — juvenile & physeal data
**Holders:** FDA (Drugs@FDA NDA 208743 nonclinical Pharm/Tox review), EMA (Eladynos EPAR), TGA
(AusPAR). **Requested:** OCR/text of toxicology narratives & appendix tables for any **juvenile-animal
study**; body/femur/tibia/humerus length; epiphyseal/physeal histology; growth-plate thickness;
premature closure; delayed ossification; recovery/washout arms; age-at-exposure and exposure
multiples. **Why needed:** these PDFs were not machine-parseable in this pass; a juvenile study, if it
exists, would be here and nowhere in the journal literature.

## 3. Human pubertal growth-plate atlas — raw/controlled-access data (GSE288028)
**Holders:** Chu/Adameyko/Chagin (Sci Transl Med 2026; bioRxiv 2025.03.14.642964); GEO GSE288028
processed matrices are public, **human raw is restricted (PII)**. **Requested:** controlled-access raw
or an author-run analysis of zonal PTH1R/GNAS/effector topology and the GH-response signature in the
two human resting-zone stem populations. **Why needed:** to validate mouse (GSE288529) resting-zone
receptor topology in fusing human physes.

## 4. Direct experiment (does not yet exist — would populate empty Tier 1)
**Design:** normal juvenile animals (ideally a fusing species) → intermittent systemic abaloparatide
vs vehicle at defined exposure multiples, dosed across a defined developmental window, **washout to
skeletal maturity**, primary endpoint **final mineralized femur/tibia length + time-to-fusion +
serial BA**; secondary resting-zone clonal/lineage readouts. **Why needed:** this single dataset would
collapse most of the model posterior and convert the strict classification from *not identified*.
