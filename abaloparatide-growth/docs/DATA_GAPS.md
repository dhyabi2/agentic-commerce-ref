# DATA GAPS

Ordered by how much each would change the answer.

## Decisive (would move the strict classification)
1. **No direct Tier-1/Tier-2 evidence exists.** No study administers systemic abaloparatide to normal
   juveniles with **conventional** long-bone physes and measures femur/tibia/humerus/body length —
   let alone to skeletal maturity after washout. Confirmed by exhaustive Europe PMC search (~400
   abaloparatide records; every "growing-animal" hit is mandibular condyle or adult metaphysis). This
   is a **genuine literature gap, not an access failure.** → strict status can only be *not identified*.
2. **Serial bone ages / time-to-fusion in the pediatric PTH(1-34) cohorts are not accessible.** Winer
   reports (PMC6298875, PMID 39883563, 20392870) give height/HAZ but not systematic BA/CA. The best
   available human "reserve-preservation / maturation-tempo" signature therefore **cannot be tested**
   from public data. (See DATA_REQUEST.md.)

## Important (would sharpen the forced estimate)
3. **FDA nonclinical Pharmacology/Toxicology review PDF** (`208743Orig1s000PharmR.pdf`) >10 MB, not
   text-extractable this pass; **EMA Eladynos EPAR** and **TGA AusPAR** PDFs were retrieved but their
   content streams were not machine-parseable. Whether a juvenile-animal study or any physeal/
   long-bone-length histology was ever submitted is **NOT confirmed** — needs manual/OCR download of
   these specific pages.
4. **Quantitative abaloparatide receptor pharmacology** — RG/R0 binding constants (Kd/IC50/fold) and
   cAMP time-course numbers (Hattersley 2016 PMID 26562265; Sato 2021 PMID 33977197) are behind
   paywall; only the qualitative "RG-selective → transient cAMP; faster recycling" is verified. The
   PK/PD model therefore uses wide priors rather than fitted kinetics.
5. **No PTH/PTHrP/teriparatide/abaloparatide perturbation transcriptomic dataset in growth-plate
   chondrocytes exists** in GEO (treated vs control). The Section-4 "empirical immediate-response
   signature → project onto zonal states" analysis is therefore **not possible**; no competence score
   was fabricated. (Closest pharmacologic perturbation available is GH ±24 h in GSE288028.)
6. **Ogawa 2002 (PMID 11862529) numeric effect sizes** (% increase in LGR/growth-plate thickness) are
   abstract-only; magnitude "NOT RETRIEVED". Direction (+) and young-only age-dependence are verified.
7. **2026 human pubertal atlas (GSE288028):** processed matrices are public but **human raw sequence
   is not** (PII). Limits independent reanalysis of human resting-zone PTH1R topology to processed
   data + published cluster-level statements.

## Structural (cannot be closed by more literature search)
8. **mRNA ≠ receptor protein ≠ cAMP ≠ growth.** No zonal PTH1R **protein** or in-vivo cAMP map of a
   conventional physis exists; the reserve question is inherently a protein/functional question that
   baseline transcriptomics cannot settle.
9. **GSE288529 is one library / one animal** (4-wk female mouse). All "fold differences" are
   descriptive; no biological-replicate CI is possible (cells ≠ biological replicates).
10. **No human PTHLH duplication / gain-of-function stature phenotype** was found — the inverted-U's
    right-hand ligand arm is anchored only by receptor gain (Jansen), not ligand gain.
