# GnRH agonist  (intervention evidence card)

- **Modality:** peptide analogue
- **Target:** GnRH receptor
- **Action:** agonist (pituitary desensitization)
- **Approval:** approved (CPP)
- **Route:** SC/IM depot or implant
- **PK:** depot 1-6 mo

## Surrogate vs final height
- **short adolescents, normal puberty (ISS-like)** (RCT to adult height): surrogate=delays BA/puberty; adult height=+4.2 cm/+0.6 SD vs placebo; **translated=YES** [PMID 12621135]
- **early-onset central precocious puberty** (cohorts/observational): surrogate=halts BA advance; PAH rises; adult height=AH > initial PAH by ~3.9 cm; reaches target; **translated=YES** [PMC5752503]
- **early-puberty girls, short PAH** (RCTs/cohorts): surrogate=additive PAH/velocity; adult height=modest AH gain over GnRHa alone; inconsistent; **translated=PARTIAL** [PMC6332953]

_Source provenance: PMID 12621135; PMC5752503_
