# Aromatase inhibitor  (intervention evidence card)

- **Modality:** small molecule
- **Target:** aromatase (CYP19)
- **Action:** inhibitor (blocks estrogen synthesis)
- **Approval:** approved oncology; OFF-LABEL short stature
- **Route:** oral daily
- **PK:** letrozole t1/2 ~2 d (UNVERIFIED)

## Surrogate vs final height
- **short/ISS boys** (double-blind RCT 2 yr): surrogate=PAH +5.9 cm; HtSDS-for-BA +0.7; adult height=NOT MEASURED (PAH only); **translated=UNKNOWN** [PMID 16189252]
- **ISS boys** (RCT to ADULT height): surrogate=(PAH gain at baseline); adult height=AH 164.8 vs 163.7 cm; P=0.49 NS; **translated=NO** [PMID 31024444]

_Source provenance: PMID 16189252; 31024444_
