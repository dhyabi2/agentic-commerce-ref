# Aromatase inhibitor — intervention evidence card

- **Modality:** small molecule  
- **Target/action:** aromatase (CYP19) / inhibitor (blocks estrogen synthesis)  
- **Approval:** approved oncology; OFF-LABEL short stature  · **Route:** oral daily  · **PK:** letrozole t1/2 ~2 d (UNVERIFIED)

## Surrogate vs ATTAINED final height
- short/ISS boys (double-blind RCT 2 yr): surrogate=PAH +5.9 cm; HtSDS-for-BA +0.7; adult height=NOT MEASURED (PAH only); **translated=UNKNOWN** [PMID 16189252]
- ISS boys (RCT to ADULT height): surrogate=(PAH gain at baseline); adult height=AH 164.8 vs 163.7 cm; P=0.49 NS; **translated=NO** [PMID 31024444]

## Interactions (pathway convergence; blank=untested, NOT safe/additive)
- Aromatase inhibitor × Estrogen (low-dose): same-target/pathway-redundant
- Aromatase inhibitor × GnRH agonist: shared-downstream (GnRHa lowers sex steroids -> delays fusion)
- Aromatase inhibitor × Long-acting GH: shared-downstream (estrogen modulates GH/IGF + fusion)
- Aromatase inhibitor × Mecasermin (rhIGF-1): shared-downstream (estrogen modulates GH/IGF + fusion)
