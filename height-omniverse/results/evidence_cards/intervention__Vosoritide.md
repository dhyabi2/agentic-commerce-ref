# Vosoritide — intervention evidence card

- **Modality:** peptide (CNP analogue)  
- **Target/action:** NPR-B (NPR2) / agonist (inhibits FGFR3/MAPK)  
- **Approval:** approved (achondroplasia)  · **Route:** SC daily  · **PK:** short t1/2 (engineered)

## Surrogate vs ATTAINED final height
- achondroplasia (phase-3 double-blind RCT 52 wk): surrogate=AGV +1.57 cm/yr (P<0.0001); adult height=NOT in RCT; ~13.6 cm/8 yr vs natural history (press, non-peer-reviewed); **translated=UNKNOWN** [PMID 32891212]

## Interactions (pathway convergence; blank=untested, NOT safe/additive)
- Abaloparatide × Vosoritide: distinct (both delay hypertrophy, different receptors)
- Infigratinib × Vosoritide: shared-downstream (MAPK braking; CNP inhibits FGFR3/MAPK)
- TYRA-300 × Vosoritide: shared-downstream (MAPK braking; CNP inhibits FGFR3/MAPK)
