# Estrogen (low-dose)  (intervention evidence card)

- **Modality:** steroid hormone
- **Target:** estrogen receptor
- **Action:** agonist (growth-plate + GH/IGF axis)
- **Approval:** approved (Turner induction)
- **Route:** oral/transdermal
- **PK:** NOT RETRIEVED

## Surrogate vs final height
- **Turner syndrome** (double-blind 2x2 RCT to adult height): surrogate=modest velocity; adult height=GH +0.78 SDS (5.0 cm); estrogen-alone small; GH+estrogen best; **translated=YES** [PMID 21449786]
