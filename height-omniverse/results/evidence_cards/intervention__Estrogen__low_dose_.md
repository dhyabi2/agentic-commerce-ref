# Estrogen (low-dose) — intervention evidence card

- **Modality:** steroid hormone  
- **Target/action:** estrogen receptor / agonist (growth-plate + GH/IGF axis)  
- **Approval:** approved (Turner induction)  · **Route:** oral/transdermal  · **PK:** NOT RETRIEVED

## Surrogate vs ATTAINED final height
- Turner syndrome (double-blind 2x2 RCT to adult height): surrogate=modest velocity; adult height=GH +0.78 SDS (5.0 cm); estrogen-alone small; GH+estrogen best; **translated=YES** [PMID 21449786]

## Interactions (pathway convergence; blank=untested, NOT safe/additive)
- Aromatase inhibitor × Estrogen (low-dose): same-target/pathway-redundant
- Estrogen (low-dose) × GnRH agonist: shared-downstream (GnRHa lowers sex steroids -> delays fusion)
- Estrogen (low-dose) × Long-acting GH: shared-downstream (estrogen modulates GH/IGF + fusion)
- Estrogen (low-dose) × Mecasermin (rhIGF-1): shared-downstream (estrogen modulates GH/IGF + fusion)
